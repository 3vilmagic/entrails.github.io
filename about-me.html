Hello, and welcome to Entrails!

My name is Kayla. I'm a twenty-two year old writer, web developer, and teacher from Brooklyn, New York. I express my love for both STEM and the humanities through my writing, weaving together themes of technology, the divine, and the dark sides of the human spirit. My work is often raw and emotional. Even when it's painful, I dig my fingers into my own psyche to find the right words.

I have always struggled with having too many hobbies, most of them fleeting and completely unrelated to one another. This website is a place for me to showcase my writing, as well as other various projects such as art and simple browser games. (I also hide ARG-esque secrets for the discerning visitor... Why don't you try and find them?)

Currently living in Arizona with my partner and friends, I work as an early childhood teacher and am going back to school for her Master's degree in Education. When I'm not working or writing, I enjoy reading books, thrifting old things, discovering new music, and bothering my cats.
