Kayla is a twenty-two year old teacher, web developer, and writer from New York City. She shows her love for both STEM and the humanities in her writing, often weaving together themes of technology, the divine, and the dark sides of the human spirit. Her work is raw and emotional. This is why she chooses to title her portfolio "Entrails"-- Even when it's painful, she digs into her own psyche to the right words

Kayla has always struggled with having too many hobbies, most of them fleeting and completely unrelated to one another. This website is a place for Kayla to showcase her writing, as well as other various projects such as art and simple browser games. She also hides ARG-esque secrets for the discerning visitor... Why don't you try and find them?

Currently living in Arizona with her partner and friends, Kayla works as an early childhood teacher and is going back to school for her Master's degree in Education. When she isn't working or writing, she enjoys reading books, thrifting old things, discovering new music, and bothering her cats.
